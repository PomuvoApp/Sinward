Sinward is designed to be private. This policy explains what the app stores, what it shares, and the choices you have.

What stays on your device

Your reflections, journal entries, written notes, chosen practices, saved Scripture and preferences are stored only on your device. We do not receive, store or have access to them. When you describe a situation in your own words, that text is used only on your device to suggest relevant topics.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. When you start, renew, restore or cancel a subscription, RevenueCat receives purchase information from Apple (such as the product, price, purchase date and expiry date) together with an anonymous identifier created by the app. This is used only to confirm whether your subscription is active. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are only turned on if you choose, and by default they never display your struggles or journal text on the lock screen.

What we don’t do

  • No account is required.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We never sell or share your data.
  • We do not collect analytics about what you write or read.

Your choices

  • Export: in Sinward, go to Settings, Privacy, Export my data, to save a copy of everything the app has stored.
  • Delete: Settings, Privacy, Delete all data permanently removes it from your device. Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.

Children

Sinward is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. For material changes, we will also let you know in the app.
